JOBHUNTERX — NATIONWIDE MULTI-SOURCE DISCOVERY
==============================================

Primary free/free-tier sources:
- JobsPipe
- Adzuna
- Jooble U.S.
- USAJOBS

Direct employer sources:
- Greenhouse
- Lever
- Ashby
- SmartRecruiters
- Public employer career pages exposing JobPosting JSON-LD

Optional paid fallbacks:
- TheirStack
- SerpAPI Google Jobs

Use the UI: Settings & Sources -> Free APIs.
Paste whichever keys you have. Missing keys do not stop configured sources.

Default targeting is the entire United States with Remote + Hybrid + On-site enabled.

Sponsorship Intelligence scores explicit visa language and can optionally sync official
U.S. Department of Labor OFLC LCA disclosure data to build a local historical sponsor index.
Historical filings are a signal, not a guarantee of sponsorship for a specific job.

Discovery can be broad, but JobHunterX does not bypass protected job-board controls,
CAPTCHA, MFA, login barriers, or legal attestations.
